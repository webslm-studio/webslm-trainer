# Harbor Bike Repair: fictional sample policy

- Pickup: customers should collect a completed bike within seven days of notification.
- E-bikes: mechanical repairs are accepted; battery and charger diagnosis or repair are not offered.
- Cancellations: please cancel at least 24 hours before an appointment.
- The shop does not ship bicycles or rent helmets.
